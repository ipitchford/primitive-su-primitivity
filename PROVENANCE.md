## Publication revision

Version `1.1.0-candidate` is intended to make bounded, review-responsive
changes:

1. retitle the manuscript for $SU(2n)_{2n}$ discoverability;
2. make the physical-to-categorical induction bridge self-contained;
3. name the resolution, charge-maximum, and electron-pair lemmas;
4. attribute the exact counting formula and close quotient-counting precedents;
5. state explicitly why Abelian dressing does not evade the obstructions;
6. clarify the bounded diagnostic so orbit invariance is not reported as
   pointwise electron fixedness;
7. add the $N=2$ boundary check and explicit code input contracts; and
8. retain an elementary reusable rank--electron obstruction and the corollary
   $R_0-R_q\ge N/2$ without claiming a new counting theory; and
9. add public claim, source, assurance, rights, and machine-navigation records.
