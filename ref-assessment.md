# Historical prospective quality assessment

This record preserves the supplied review's prospective assessment of the
original manuscript and states how the revision affects that evidence. It is
not an official REF decision, a correctness probability, journal acceptance,
or human peer review. The review was AI-assisted and used REF 2021 Main Panel B
criteria only as an available benchmark while final REF 2029 guidance was not
yet published.

## Preserved assessment

| Dimension | Central assessment | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Originality | 2-star | Established family, resolution machinery, and charge counts; residual originality lies in their targeted all-sector assembly and electron-pair conclusion |
| Significance | 2-star | Closes an explicit infinite-family proof gap, but broader durable influence was not established |
| Rigour | 3-star | Coherent universal argument and strong exact diagnostics, subject at review time to expositional, attribution, and auxiliary-output repairs |
| Overall | 2-star | Holistic judgement, not an arithmetic average |

The credible specialist uncertainty is between 2-star and 3-star. A specialist
could place greater weight on resolving the explicitly deferred all-parameter
problem, but the bounded evidence did not establish field-wide or lasting
importance strongly enough to make 3-star the central judgement. No supported
4-star case was identified: the work was not shown to be field-leading,
agenda-setting, or a comparable conceptual advance.

## Effect of revision

Version `1.1.0-candidate` closes the review's four major and seven minor
comments. It makes the induction bridge explicit, correctly attributes the
exact charge formula and strict maximum, treats Abelian dressing, replaces the
over-strong diagnostic label, strengthens the rank result to
$R_0-R_q\ge N/2$, and retains an elementary rank--electron criterion.

These changes improve correctness exposition, attribution, reuse, and
assurance. They do not by themselves demonstrate broader significance or
methodological novelty. The quantitative gap is an elementary corollary of
established exact counts and the resolved-rank identity; the obstruction lemma
is a transparent abstraction of the originating factor definition. The
historical central 2-star assessment, credible 3-star uncertainty, and absence
of a 4-star basis are therefore preserved rather than upgraded.

The supplied review binds the original reviewed bytes, not every later edit.
The revision has received targeted internal mathematical, source, and
computational checks, but no independent human peer review or official REF
assessment. Future specialist review, new applications, or closer prior art
could change the prospective judgement; this package does not pre-empt those
