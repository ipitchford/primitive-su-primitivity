# Source record

This file records the primary texts and exact passages used in the 11 October
2026 publication intake. Search results were discovery aids only; technical
conclusions below come from the cited full texts. The detailed private search
payload is not distributed.

## Primary sources inspected in the publication intake

### Gao et al., `SC-sharp`, arXiv:2610.10679v1

- URL: <https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10679v1>
- Inspected: Sections IV.5 and VII.4; closing open-directions paragraph.
- Used for: target primitivity definition, charge-one construction, fine
  sectors, proposed $SU(2n)_{2n}$ family, and the explicit deferred-proof gap.
- Boundary: this source proposes the result; it is not evidence that no later
  or simultaneous proof exists.

### Gao et al., `Topological Charge-2ne Superconductors`, arXiv:2512.21325v4

- URL: <https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21325v4>
- Used for: originating family and the later electron-retaining fixed-point
  convention.
- Boundary: the public package uses version 4; an earlier version's $N=4$
  fixed-point count is not the operative convention.

### Elashvili, Jibladze, and Pataraia (1999)

- Title: *Combinatorics of necklaces and "Hermite reciprocity"*.
- Full text: <https://rmi.tsu.ge/~jib/pubs/elajipa.pdf>
- Inspected: p. 174 equation (2); pp. 176-177 Theorem 1 and equation (4);
  p. 187 Proposition 8.
- Used for: the exact all-charge weak-composition formula, its roots-of-unity
  derivation, and the strict neutral maximum.
- Boundary: these are exact prior-art collisions, not new formulas of this
  package.

### Muratovic-Ribic and Wang, arXiv:1305.3259v1

- Title: *The multisubset sum problem for finite abelian groups*.
- Full text: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1305.3259>
- Inspected: Theorem 1, p. 2.
- Used for: a second exact finite-group-character route to the charge count.

### Brualdi and Newman (1970)

- Title: *An enumeration problem for a congruence equation*.
- Full text:
  <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/74B/jresv74Bn1p37_A1b.pdf>
- Inspected: pp. 37-39, especially the main theorem.
- Used for: the exact neutral weak-composition count.
- Boundary: it does not state the all-charge or superconducting theorem.

### Fuchs, Runkel, and Schweigert, hep-th/0403157

- Title: *TFT construction of RCFT correlators III: Simple currents*.
- Full text: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0403157>
- Inspected: Remark 3.19(i); Lemma 4.6; Proposition 4.7; Definition 4.8;
  Remark 4.10.
- Used for: orbit equivalence, disjoint constituents, and the twisted
  stabiliser endomorphism algebra.
- Boundary: cyclic cocycle linearisation is a separate elementary step, not a
  verbatim statement of Lemma 4.6.

### Closset, Furrer, Keyes, and Khlaif, arXiv:2501.11665v3

- Full text: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.11665v3>
- Inspected: Section 3.1.1 equations (3.14)-(3.25); the three condensation
  steps in Section 3.1.2; Section 3.1.4 equations (3.39)-(3.47).
- Used for: close simple-current orbit, stabiliser, charge, and
  Jordan-totient quotient-counting machinery; level-convention comparison.
- Boundary: at $k=N$, $K=2N$, $r=N/2$, the printed quotient count groups
  $R_0+R_{N/2}$ rather than resolving all fine charges.

### Closset, Furrer, and Khlaif, arXiv:2405.18141v4
