# Bounded prior-art position

Audit date: 11 October 2026. This is a formula-first, bounded comparison. It
is not an exhaustive novelty search, a historical-priority certificate, or a
correctness review.

## Residual contribution

The supported contribution language is:

> We give a uniform proof of the primitivity proposed by Gao et al., using
> established cyclic-resolution and charge-counting results together with an
> explicit electron-pair obstruction in every nonzero flux sector.

The family, charge-one construction, definition of primitivity, and proposed
all-parameter property are due to Gao and collaborators. The exact
unrestricted charge-counting formula, its roots-of-unity route, and the strict
zero-residue maximum are older mathematics. Cyclic fixed-point resolution and
closely related Jordan-totient quotient counts are also established.

The bounded search did not locate an earlier complete statement combining
every individual electron-retaining fine-sector inequality with an explicit
electron pair to prove the full proposed primitivity property. That supports a
narrow application claim, not `first`, `new family`, `new counting theory`, or
absolute historical priority.

## Exact and close predecessors

### Originating construction

Gao et al., arXiv:2610.10679v1, Sections IV.5 and VII.4, define the target
primitivity notion, propose the $SU(2n)_{2n}$ family, give the $n=2,3$
illustrations, and defer a rigorous all-parameter proof. The earlier
charge-$2ne$ construction is in arXiv:2512.21325v4.

### Charge counts

Elashvili, Jibladze, and Pataraia (1999), Theorem 1 and equation (4), give the
manuscript's unrestricted $A_L(r)$ formula after the specialisation
$n=m=L$, $k=r$. Their proof already uses roots of unity. Proposition 8 makes
$A_L(0)>A_L(r)$ for $r\ne0$ an immediate nonnegative-divisor corollary.
Muratovic-Ribic and Wang, Theorem 1, give the same count from finite abelian
group characters. Brualdi and Newman give the exact neutral count.

The manuscript retains a short derivation for readability but does not claim
these formulas or the strict maximum as new.

### Resolution and quotient counts

Fuchs, Runkel, and Schweigert, Lemma 4.6 and Proposition 4.7, establish orbit
separation and identify the induced-module endomorphism algebra with a twisted
stabiliser group algebra. The further cyclic linearisation step is elementary
and stated separately. Bantay supplies the general untwisted-stabiliser caveat.

Closset and collaborators provide close charge/orbit/stabiliser/Jordan
quotient machinery. Under their convention, this manuscript corresponds to
$k=N$, supersymmetric level $K=2N$, and quotient order $r=N/2$. Their printed
count groups the fine sectors as $R_0+R_{N/2}$ rather than establishing every
individual inequality $R_q<R_0$. It gives $14=8+6$ at $N=4$ and
$60=32+28$ at $N=6$.

## Bounded extensions

The reusable rank-plus-electron obstruction is a transparent abstraction of
the source definition: rank mismatch excludes the two-object factor, while a
nontrivial electron two-cycle excludes the one-object electron-fixed factor.
It also covers Abelian dressing because invertible dressing preserves the
relevant rank and electron action. This is useful packaging, not a major new
categorical theory.

The quantitative corollary $R_0-R_q\ge N/2$ follows by applying the integer
gap of at least one from the known charge decomposition to every divisor term,
then using $\sum_{d\mid m}J_2(d)=m^2$ with $m=N/2$. It is an elementary
corollary of prior counting plus the manuscript's resolved-rank formula. No
general equality classification is claimed.

## Search boundary

The audit covered supplied dated records, a live general web index, direct
versioned arXiv full texts, an author-hosted 1999 paper, and the NIST original.
It did not exhaust MathSciNet, zbMATH, Google Scholar citation graphs, private
correspondence, unindexed repositories, or non-English literature. The
originating proposal was four days old at the search date, so indexing delay
and simultaneous work are material. No author was contacted.
