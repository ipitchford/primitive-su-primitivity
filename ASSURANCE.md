# Assurance statement

This package combines a written universal proof, historical internal audits, a
supplied AI-assisted review, bounded exact computation, and a bounded prior-art
comparison. These are different evidence types and must not be collapsed into
a single confidence score.

## Mathematical claim boundary

The result concerns the unitary $SU(2n)_{2n}$ WZW modular category and the
specified charge-one fermionic simple-current construction. It retains the
physical electron, uses all resolved $A$-modules rather than only local
modules, and adopts the originating work's charge grading and
electron-compatible definition of primitivity. Changing the charge assignment,
the factorisation convention, or the sector model would require a new proof
audit.

The proof uses only object counts, cyclic stabiliser resolution, the charge
grading, and an explicit electron pair. It does not construct a microscopic
realisation, compute all fusion rules or crossed braiding, classify all
superconducting phases, or produce an experimental prediction.

## Computational boundary

The public programs use exact integer arithmetic to enumerate bounded label
orbits and evaluate the divisor formula. Under CPython 3.13.5, seven tests pass
in normal and optimized modes, two public evidence files replay byte for byte,
and an independent verifier checks 5,050 charge cells, ranks through even
$N=100$, the quantitative gap through that range, and five semantic negative
controls. They support convention checking and implementation replay. They are
not an induction argument and do not validate the physical-to-categorical
bridge. The historical `electron_fixed` label was specifically challenged as
too strong for what its computation alone showed; the release uses
`resolved_objects_over_electron_invariant_parent_orbits` and does not infer
pointwise electron fixedness from parent-orbit invariance.

## Review and contribution boundary

The supplied report recommended minor revisions. Its prospective dimension
judgements were originality 2-star, significance 2-star, rigour 3-star, and
overall 2-star, with a natural 2-to-3-star specialist uncertainty and no
supported 4-star case. The dimensions are qualitative and are not averaged.
The report is AI-assisted, third-party `NOASSERTION` material, not human peer
review or an official assessment.

The retained reusable obstruction lemma abstracts the same two-obstruction
logic. The quantitative bound $R_0-R_q\ge N/2$ is an elementary corollary of
the cited integer charge gap and the resolved-rank formula. Neither addition
automatically shows broader influence, methodological novelty, or a quality
uplift.
